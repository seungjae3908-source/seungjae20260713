## Loss diagnostics

Completed closes strictly before final exit, plus entry price, determine the
observed close-MFE/MAE. The final candle close/high may occur after an intrabar
stop, so it is excluded. These are censored lower-resolution diagnostics, not
proof there was no intrabar favorable move. Group losing trades below +0.5R,
+0.5R..<+1R, and >=+1R observed close progress. The groups are never fed to signal
selection. Retain gross/net/cost reconciliation, concentration and all losers.

## Preliminary finding and limitations

Local results support investigating full-size runner exits for first retests in
both observed windows, but NOT universal improvement. Fixed +2R exits harmed
results; time stops helped the prior year but harmed the recent window. Early
results are posted at PR comment5826258757. Exact local hash is pinned in CI;
this is reproducibility, not a gate that a return must be positive.

The prior-year full runner still relies heavily on a few winners and has greater
MDD. Win rate can FALL despite better net returns. No accuracy improvement,
monthly-profit guarantee, genuine OOS, or actual execution proof is claimed.
Static survivor universe, varying coverage, candle fills, ticks/minimum orders,
historical news/listings/float/dilution, settlement and intrabar MDD remain limits.
